AUTOMATION SAMPLES

AI-Powered Lead Qualification & Auto-Responder

A fully automated lead management system built in n8n that detects new leads, qualifies them using AI, and sends personalized follow-up emails — all within 60 seconds, without any human intervention. Built using n8n, Groq AI, Google Sheets, and Gmail.

AI-Powered Discovery Call Summary & Follow-Up System

Service-based businesses often lose momentum right after a discovery call — the recap, follow-up email, lead logging, and task creation all fall on the founder, usually 30-45 minutes per call, often late or skipped. This automation turns the moment a call ends into an instant, consistent follow-up sequence.

AI-Powered Feedback Triage & Response System

An automated pipeline that reads incoming feedback emails and uses AI to classify sentiment, urgency, and category. Non-positive or urgent cases trigger a team alert with a drafted response for human review; routine feedback logs quietly. A companion scenario tracks sent replies and marks items resolved once a human responds — closing the loop without any AI-generated message ever reaching a customer unreviewed.

Smart Inventory & Reorder Alert System

Sales inventory used to run on manual checks and guesswork, so stock ran out before anyone noticed. This automation checks inventory daily, catches low-stock and restock events automatically, and hands them to an AI Agent that ranks what's actually urgent and drafts the manager's alert — not just a flat list, but a real judgment call on what to act on first. Result: stockouts caught before they happen, zero manual checking.

CRM Funnel & Spreadsheet Sync Automation

A signup funnel with instant lead-magnet delivery and automated lead tracking via webhook into Google Sheets. Includes a diagnosed duplicate-data bug and a deliverability fix on a trial-tier setup.
